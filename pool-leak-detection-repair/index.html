Is It a Leak or Evaporation? Start With the Bucket Test

A pool loses water every day to evaporation, and heat, dry air, and wind speed it up. In southern California, hot days and the dry Santa Ana winds of fall can pull a quarter inch or more off the surface in a day. A drop that size is normal. A larger drop, or one that continues in cool, calm weather, suggests a leak.

The bucket test separates the two. Fill a bucket with pool water to about the same level as the pool, and set it on a pool step so both sit in the same sun and wind. Mark the water level inside the bucket and on the pool wall, and wait 24 hours without adding water or using the pool.

Compare the two marks. If the pool dropped more than the bucket, water is leaving through a leak. If both dropped the same, the loss is evaporation.

What to Do If You Suspect a Pool Leak

A few steps protect the pool and give the plumber what is needed.

  • 1
    Do not drain the poolDraining can let groundwater push on the shell and lift or crack it. Leave the water in unless a professional says otherwise.
  • 2
    Record the levelMark it and note the date and time. Track it for a day or two without adding water.
  • 3
    Run the equipment-off testCompare the loss with the pump running and with it off. A bigger loss with the pump on points to a pressure-side line.
  • 4
    Check the auto-fillA stuck auto-fill valve can hide a leak or cause one.
  • 5
    Keep the water meter readingA meter that runs fast relative to normal use is an extra clue.

What a Plumber Covers and What a Pool Company Covers

A leak in the pool's plumbing lines is a plumbing problem, and that is what this page covers. We find leaks in suction and return lines and in the supply to the pool, and we repair them to the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Cracked plaster or tile, a failed liner, and worn pump or filter equipment belong to a pool company. When the leak is in the shell, we will tell you so after testing, and you will have a report to give the right trade.

That honesty saves money. Fixing the wrong thing leaves the leak running, and the water bill keeps climbing. A hidden leak elsewhere on the property is covered under household leak detection. A leak in the buried supply line that feeds a pool belongs to water line repair and replacement, which is a different case from a leak in the pool's own plumbing.

How We Find a Pool Leak

  1. Confirm

    The bucket test and the level history show whether the pool is leaking.

  2. Isolate

    The equipment is turned off and each line is plugged and tested on its own.

  3. Pinpoint

    Dye and listening tools locate the leak within the failed line.

  4. Report and repair

    You get the location and the repair price, and nothing is dug up until you agree.

Private pools are part of the housing mix here, particularly on the larger lots of the hillside community of Foothill Ranch and in parts of the 92630 area. They are not on every street, so the question is mostly relevant to owners who have one.

Water drained from a pool should not go to the storm drain, so ask your city about disposal rules before any draining.
