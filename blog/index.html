What You Will Find Here

These guides cover the questions that come up before, during, and after a plumbing problem: why a water bill jumped, whether a permit is needed, what insurance pays, and how to find the shutoff when water is running. Each one uses local facts where they change the answer, such as which water district bills you and what era builders developed your neighborhood in.

The Lake Forest Pro Plumbing team writes each guide and links to the service and area pages that cover the next step. If you are in the middle of a problem, skip the reading and call the 24/7 line.

Our references include the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), district publications, and published city permit handouts.
