A City Planned From Scratch, Now Aging Together

Rancho Santa Margarita is a master-planned community that incorporated on January 1, 2000, as Orange County's 33rd city. The first homes sold in 1986, and Dove Canyon and Robinson Ranch followed. The 2020 Census counted 47,949 residents across about 13 square miles.

Because the city grew in waves from 1986 onward, many homes now share the same age. Water heaters on their second or third unit, supply lines at the end of their service life, and fixtures that have worn out are the common reasons for a call.

Repairs follow the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). One failed part is a repair. A string of them in an older home can point to repiping the house, and a tank past its life is covered under water heater replacement.

Two ZIP Codes and Two Water Districts in One City

The city's own address, City Hall at 22112 El Paseo, is in ZIP 92688. Dove Canyon, Rancho Cielo, and Robinson Ranch use 92679, the same ZIP code that covers Portola Hills and Coto de Caza. Tell the dispatcher your street instead of guessing from the ZIP.

Santa Margarita Water District serves the city for drinking water, recycled water, and wastewater, and nearly all of its water is imported through the Metropolitan Water District. Trabuco Canyon Water District also serves portions of the city. Your water bill names your district.

That matters because responsibility generally splits at the meter. A leak on the street side is the district's, and a leak on the house side is yours. For a buried line between the two, see water line repair.

Before You Call From a Gated or HOA Community

A few details save time when the plumber has to get past a gate or an association.

  • 1
    Name your communitySay whether you are in Dove Canyon, Robinson Ranch, Rancho Cielo, Trabuco Highlands, or another neighborhood. Each has its own entry and rules.
  • 2
    Leave a gate contactGive the dispatcher a name and a phone number that will answer the gate. A plumber stuck at the entry loses time while water runs.
  • 3
    Ask who owns the shutoffIn an attached home the valve may be shared. Your association manager can say which one to close.
  • 4
    Photograph the damageTake photos and video before cleanup. Your insurer and your association may both ask for them.
  • 5
    Read the meterPhotograph it, then again after an hour with everything off. Movement means water is still escaping.

Parks, the Lake, and the Roads We Use to Reach You

The city maintains 21 parks on 154 acres, including Central Park in Trabuco Highlands, Trabuco Mesa Park, and Solano Park. Lago Santa Margarita is an 11.5-acre lake with a path around it, and the Bell Tower Regional Community Center is the city's recreation hub.

O'Neill Regional Park lies along Trabuco Creek just outside the city, and Tijeras Creek Golf Club is a public course. Santa Margarita Parkway and Antonio Parkway are the main roads, and the city sits on the Plano Trabuco plateau beside the Cleveland National Forest.

Lake Forest is about five miles to the west-northwest in a straight line. Neighbors in Trabuco Canyon and Coto de Caza use the same line, and the homepage covers the whole area at emergency plumber in Lake Forest.
