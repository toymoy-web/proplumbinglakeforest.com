One Water District: Irvine Ranch Water District

Irvine Ranch Water District supplies Irvine, plus parts of Lake Forest, Newport Beach, Tustin, Costa Mesa, Orange, and unincorporated Orange County. It was founded in 1961, began recycled water service in 1967, and adopted a tiered, budget-based rate structure in 1991.

In practice, a leak that raises your bill may show up in a higher tier. A meter test tells you whether water is moving with everything off, and the district sets what it offers after a documented repair.

Responsibility splits at the meter, and work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). For a hidden leak in the house, leak detection finds it without opening walls.
