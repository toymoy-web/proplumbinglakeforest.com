One Fixture or the Whole House? Finding the Clog

Where a clog sits decides how urgent it is. A single slow sink usually points to the trap or the branch line serving that fixture, which is a short, simple job. Several fixtures slowing together point to the main line, which carries everything out of the house.

The clearest sign of a main-line blockage is water appearing where it should not. A toilet bowl that rises when the washing machine drains, or a shower that gurgles when someone flushes, means the main is restricted.

Tell the dispatcher which fixtures are affected and on which floor. That one detail tells the plumber which tools to bring.

Why Older Lake Forest Sewer Laterals Clog

The sewer lateral is the pipe that runs from your house to the main in the street. In California the property owner is generally responsible for the lateral, and the water district that collects wastewater is responsible for the main. If several neighbors back up at the same time, the district should hear about it.

What to Do and What Not to Do While You Wait

What you do in the first twenty minutes can keep a messy backup from turning into a flooded floor.

  • 1
    Stop all water usePause the washing machine and dishwasher and skip flushing. Every gallon you add has to go somewhere.
  • 2
    Skip chemical drain openersThey can sit in a stopped pipe and become a hazard to the person who opens it. A plumber also cannot tell what is in the water.
  • 3
    Do not plunge a backing-up mainPlunging pushes wastewater toward the lowest opening and can splash contaminated water.
  • 4
    Find the cleanoutMany homes have a capped pipe near the foundation or in a side yard. Telling the plumber where it is saves time.
  • 5
    Keep people and pets awayIf wastewater reached the floor, keep children and pets out of the area and wash hands after touching anything wet.

How We Clear a Blocked Drain

Cable machines punch through most clogs. Grease and scale need more, because a cable leaves a hole through the middle and the walls stay coated. That is where hydro jetting comes in.

  1. Locate

    Find the cleanout or the nearest access and confirm which fixtures are affected.

  2. Clear

    Run a cable for a standard blockage or use high-pressure jetting to scour grease and scale from the pipe walls.

  3. Look

    Send a camera down the line when the clog is in the main or keeps returning.

  4. Report

    You get a plain account of what was found and whether the line needs any repair.
